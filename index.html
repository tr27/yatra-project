What is the weather like? Is it very cold?

Temperatures can drop significantly at high altitudes like Kedarnath and Badrinath. Expect 5-15°C during the day and near freezing (0-5°C) at night. Heavy, layered thermal clothing and windcheaters are strictly mandatory regardless of the month you travel.

What about walking and physical fitness?

The Kedarnath trek is roughly 16-22 km (depending on the starting point) and requires good physical stamina. Yamunotri involves a 6 km steep trek. Ponies and palanquins are readily available for hire. Badrinath and Gangotri are easily accessible by road.

What mandatory documents do I need?

Every pilgrim must carry a valid original Aadhar Card (or Passport for foreign nationals), the Government Yatra Registration E-Pass (which we will process for you), and a recent Medical Fitness Certificate confirming you are fit for high-altitude travel.

What is the Booking & Cancellation Procedure?

A 30% advance secures your booking and transport. A 40% installment is due 30 days before departure, and the remaining balance upon arrival. Cancellations made 30+ days prior receive a 75% refund. Cancellations within 15 days of travel are strictly non-refundable.
